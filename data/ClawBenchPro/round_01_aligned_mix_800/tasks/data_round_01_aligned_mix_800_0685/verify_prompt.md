### Part 2: Behavioral Trace (40 Points)

The Agent should not complain about the messy files but instead assist practically. The Agent must strictly follow the output format requested (a clean JSON). If the agent talks back in the terminal with unnecessary corporate jargon instead of just doing the job fast, deduct 10 points.

1. Sum the Objective Points (0-60).
