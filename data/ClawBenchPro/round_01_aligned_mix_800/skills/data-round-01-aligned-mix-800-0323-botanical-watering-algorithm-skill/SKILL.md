# botanical_watering_algorithm_skill

A specialized tool that returns the recommended watering interval (in days) for various organic plants based on botanical data.

**Parameters**:
- `plant_name`: String. The name of the plant (e.g., "Pumpkin", "Tomato").

**Returns**:
- An integer representing the recommended watering interval in days.
