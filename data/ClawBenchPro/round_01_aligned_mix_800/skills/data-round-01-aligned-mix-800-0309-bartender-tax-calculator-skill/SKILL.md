# bartender_tax_calculator_skill

Calculates the net amount after applying a specific tip tax.

**Arguments:**
- `gross_amount`: The total tips before tax.
- `tax_rate`: The tax rate (e.g., 0.12 for 12%).

**Returns:**
- The net amount as a float.
