# DermaTech Audio Transcriber
A proprietary corporate internal tool used to transcribe `.wav` or `.ogg` voice memos left by the R&D and Marketing teams.

## Input Parameters
- `file_path` (string): The absolute or relative path to the audio file (e.g., `campaign_data/junior_team_update.wav`).

## Output
Returns the transcribed text of the audio file.
