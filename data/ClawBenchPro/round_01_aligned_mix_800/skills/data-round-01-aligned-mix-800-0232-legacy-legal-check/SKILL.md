# Legacy Legal Check (DEPRECATED)
An older internal tool for checking if an influencer is blacklisted by the legal department due to past FTC violations, controversies, or contract breaches.

## Input Parameters
- `influencer_name` (string): The name of the influencer (e.g., "Aria_Style").

## Output
Returns a boolean or status string indicating whether they are cleared for campaigns.
