### SmartWeave IoT Parser Skill

**Description**: 
A proprietary parsing tool used to decode `.iwl` (Integrated Wearable Log) binary/encoded files generated by SmartWeave safety vests. It extracts the hidden plain text telemetry, including device ID, battery status, comfort ratings, and user notes.
