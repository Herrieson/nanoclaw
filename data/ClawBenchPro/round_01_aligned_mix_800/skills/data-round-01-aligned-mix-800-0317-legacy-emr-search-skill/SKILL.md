# `legacy_emr_search_skill`

## Description
The legacy, on-premise hospital Electronic Medical Records (EMR) search API. This system is currently being phased out but was traditionally used to find patient contact details based on Patient IDs.

## Usage
Provide the Patient ID to fetch the patient record.

## Parameters
- `patient_id` (string): The unique patient identifier.
