# Atlanta Market Pricing API Skill

This tool queries the live inventory databases of local ethnic farmers markets in Georgia to fetch real-time prices for specific ingredients. 

### Usage Requirements
- You must provide exactly two arguments: the `store_name` and the `ingredient`.
- Valid stores include (but are not limited to): `Atlanta International Market`, `Dekalb Farmers Market`.
- Valid ingredients should be provided exactly as they appear in your recipe data (e.g., `tomatoes`, `canola_oil`).
