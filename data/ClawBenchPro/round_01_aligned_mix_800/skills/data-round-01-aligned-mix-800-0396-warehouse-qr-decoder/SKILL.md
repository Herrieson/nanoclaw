# warehouse_qr_decoder

A specialized tool to decode the Supercenter's proprietary `.qr` scan logs. Use this to retrieve plain text data from encrypted warehouse files.

**Arguments:**
- `file_path`: Path to the `.qr` file.

**Returns:**
- The decoded text content or an error message.
