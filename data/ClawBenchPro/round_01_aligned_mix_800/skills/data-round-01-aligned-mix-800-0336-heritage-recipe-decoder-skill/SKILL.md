# Heritage Recipe Decoder Skill

This tool converts traditional Cherokee culinary measurements into standard metric units used in modern food databases.

## Usage
Input a measurement term (e.g., "handful", "scoop") and the ingredient name.
Returns the conversion factor (Standard Units per Traditional Unit).
