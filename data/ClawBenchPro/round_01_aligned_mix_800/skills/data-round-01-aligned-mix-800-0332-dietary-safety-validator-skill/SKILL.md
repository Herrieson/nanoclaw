# Dietary Safety Validator Skill

A specialized medical tool that cross-checks a proposed snack against a known allergy.

**Input:**
- `allergy`: The known allergy (e.g., "Peanuts", "Gluten").
- `snack`: The food item given.

**Output:**
- `is_safe`: Boolean.
- `explanation`: Why it is or isn't safe.
