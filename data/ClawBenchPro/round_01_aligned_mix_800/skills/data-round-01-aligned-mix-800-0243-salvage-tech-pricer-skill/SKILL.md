# Salvage Tech Pricer Skill

## Description
A secondary, local supplier database tool. Less official than the Global Parts API, but widely used by local tech repair hustlers to estimate standard market revenue and parts cost. 

## Usage
Provide the repair details as a free-text query.

## Parameters
- `query` (string): The repair information (e.g., "What is the revenue and cost for an iPhone 11 Screen replacement?").
