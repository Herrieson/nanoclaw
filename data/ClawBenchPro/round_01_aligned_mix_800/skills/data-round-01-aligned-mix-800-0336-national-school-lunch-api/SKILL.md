# National School Lunch Compliance API

Validates if a proposed meal's cost and calories meet federal guidelines for the National School Lunch Program.

## Parameters
- `meal_name`: Name of the meal.
- `cost_per_serving`: Total calculated cost.
- `calories_per_serving`: Total calculated calories.
