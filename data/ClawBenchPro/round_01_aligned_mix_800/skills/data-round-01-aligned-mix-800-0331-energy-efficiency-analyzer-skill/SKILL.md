# Skill: Energy Efficiency Analyzer

## Description
A professional tool that calculates if a property unit is "High-Energy" based on its average monthly wattage and insulation index.

## Usage
Input: `wattage` (float), `insulation_index` (float)
Output: `{"status": "High-Energy" | "Low-Energy", "recommendation": "string"}`
